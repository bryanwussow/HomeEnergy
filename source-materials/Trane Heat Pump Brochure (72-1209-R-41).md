## Feature Comparison (Premier / Priority / Choice tiers)
- **Compressor speeds:** 20/18 TruComfort = Variable; 17 Multi-Speed = Multi; 16/15/14 = Single
- **Capacity variability:** 20 TruComfort 25–100% (1/10 of 1% increments); 18 TruComfort 25–100% (1% increments); 17 Multi-Speed 70/100%; single-stage models 100% only
- **Sound power level [dB(A)]:** 20 TruComfort 51–75; 18 TruComfort 54–76; 17 Multi-Speed 63–75; single-stage 70–72
- **Trane Link connectivity, ComfortSeek, Compressor Sound Enclosure, WeatherGuard top:** vary by tier — see original PDF table for exact checkmarks per model
- **Warranty:** 20/18 TruComfort get a 12-year limited compressor / 10-year outdoor coil & functional parts warranty (with registration); other tiers get a 10-year limited compressor/coil/functional parts warranty (with registration)

## Relevance to This Project
The Energy Saver NC audit's "Goal" heating system swaps the existing 1999 central heat pump (6.8 HSPF / 11 SEER) for a new higher-efficiency unit (8.2 HSPF2 / 15.2 SEER2 as modeled in the audit's Goal scenario). This Trane lineup is one air-source option being considered as an alternative/comparison to the WaterFurnace geothermal options — a Trane 16 or 17 series (17.0–18.0 SEER2, 8.5–11.0 HSPF2) would be the rough efficiency tier matching or exceeding the audit's Goal assumption; the 18/20 TruComfort models would exceed it.
