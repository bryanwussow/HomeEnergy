## Glossary (selected terms relevant to analysis)
- **AFUE** — Annual Fuel Utilization Efficiency (heating equipment).
- **HSPF** — Heating Seasonal Performance Factor (Btu delivered per watt-hr for heat pump heating).
- **SEER** — Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio (cooling).
- **EER** — Energy Efficiency Ratio (cooling capacity Btu/hr ÷ watts at a specific outdoor temp).
- **EF** — Energy Factor (water heater/appliance efficiency measure).
- **SIR** — Savings to Investment Ratio; ≥1 means savings pay for the upgrade before it needs replacing again.
- **MIRR** — Modified Internal Rate of Return; equivalent bank-account interest rate for the investment.
- **Payback Period** — time for savings to equal system cost; this report's calc uses 3% annual inflation and 5% fuel cost escalation.
- **ACH50 / NACH** — air changes per hour at 50 Pa test pressure / natural (untested) air changes per hour.
